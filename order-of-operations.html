Order of operations: the routine I literally made labels for

Oil cleanser to sunscreen, in the order I printed on my own travel labels, plus what I've actually bought along the way.

In October 2024 I made a full set of travel labels for my skincare. Forty-six pages in Canva. One label per step. Is that normal? Doesn't matter. It means the steps are written down, in order, by me, so here they are.

The steps, in label order

  1. Oil cleanser
  2. Cleanser
  3. Toner
  4. Essence
  5. Hylo acid #1, #2, #3 (yes, three. I have a whole post about that.)
  6. Vitamin C
  7. Mask
  8. Moisturizer
  9. Sunscreen
  10. Oils: jojoba and squalane

Why the order matters (the boring-but-true part)

The general rule most dermatologists and brands agree on: cleanse first, then go from the thinnest, most watery layers to the thickest. Watery toners and essences go on before serums, serums before creams, and anything meant to seal things in comes later. In the morning, sunscreen is the last skincare step so nothing dilutes it or sits on top of it. Sleeping masks are designed as the final step of a night routine.

Double cleansing (oil cleanser, then a regular cleanser) is a K-beauty staple: the oil step breaks down sunscreen and makeup, and the second cleanser washes away what's left.
