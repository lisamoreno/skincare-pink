About

Hi, I'm Lisa.

Also known as Queen Lisa. I buy a lot of skincare.

skincare.pink is my skincare education blog, for ages 25 to 95, and someday a shop. I write about products I've actually bought, which, going by my receipts from Stylevana, Sephora, YesStyle and Dermstore since 2023, is a lot of products. It's mostly K-beauty and a lot of hyaluronic acid. I also made printed labels for every step of my routine, so yes, I care about the order you put it on.

The rules here

  • Receipts or it didn't happen. If I write about a product, I bought it.
  • Order matters. Thinnest to thickest, sunscreen last in the morning.
  • No fake results. I'll tell you what I bought. I won't tell you a serum is a medical treatment.
