What hyaluronic acid actually does

Hyaluronic acid is a humectant: an ingredient that attracts and holds onto water. Your skin makes it naturally. In skincare it helps the top layer of skin hold moisture, which is why it shows up in toners, serums and cleansers alike.

  • Damp skin helps. It's usually applied to slightly damp skin (after toner is perfect), because it needs water to grab.
  • Seal it in. Follow with a moisturizer so that moisture stays put.
  • It plays well with others. HA is generally gentle and easy to layer with most other ingredients.
  • It's hydration, not a miracle. It won't replace sunscreen, and it's not a treatment for a skin condition.

"Triple" or "5" on a label usually refers to multiple forms or molecular sizes of hyaluronic acid in one formula.
