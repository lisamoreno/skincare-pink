Reading the label: SPF and PA

  • SPF is about UVB, the rays mainly behind sunburn. SPF 50 blocks more UVB than SPF 30, but no sunscreen blocks all of it.
  • PA+ to PA++++ is a rating system used in Japan and Korea for UVA protection, the rays tied to tanning and skin aging. More plus signs mean more UVA protection.
  • "Broad spectrum" is the US label term for sunscreen that's tested for UVA protection as well as UVB.

The rules nobody gets out of

  • Sunscreen is the last skincare step in the morning.
  • Use enough. Most people apply far less than the amount SPF is tested at.
  • Reapply, generally every two hours outdoors and after swimming or sweating, or as the label says.
  • A color-correcting treatment with SPF22 is nice, but it doesn't replace a real SPF 30+ sunscreen.
