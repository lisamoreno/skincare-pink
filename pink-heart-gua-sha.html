The pink heart gua sha (yes, I bought two)

A heart-shaped pink scraping board from a 2024 K-beauty haul, and some honest gua sha basics.

In July 2024, tucked into a Stylevana order between brushes and a hair mask, I bought two MissLady heart-shaped gua sha boards. Pink, obviously. Here's what gua sha is, and how to do it gently.

What gua sha is

Gua sha comes from traditional Chinese medicine. The facial version is a gentle massage: you glide a smooth, flat tool over skin, usually with an oil or serum so it doesn't drag.

The basics

  • Slip first. Apply a facial oil or serum so the tool glides instead of tugging.
  • Light pressure. This is a massage, not a scrub. Hold the tool fairly flat against your skin.
  • Long, outward strokes. Most guides go from the center of the face outward and upward, and down the neck.
  • Keep it clean. Wash the tool after you use it.
  • Skip it on irritated skin. Avoid active breakouts, broken skin and sunburn, and check with your doctor if you've had recent procedures or injectables.

What it is not: a replacement for a facelift, or a permanent fix. Any puffiness change you see is temporary, which is why people make it a habit.
